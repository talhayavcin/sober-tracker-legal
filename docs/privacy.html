Sober Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Sober Tracker (“the App”, “we”, “us”) is built to be private by design. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, where it is stored and the choices you have.

In short: everything you enter about your journey stays on your device. There are no accounts, and we cannot see your check-ins, journal entries or letters.

Information stored only on your device

The following information is saved locally on your device and is never uploaded to our servers:

  • Your start date, drinking habits, weekly spending estimate, currency, motivations and goals
  • Daily check-ins, including mood, cravings, tags and drink days
  • Journal entries, letters to your future self and savings goals
  • SOS sessions, earned badges and app settings such as reminders and appearance

Your device’s own backups (for example iCloud or Google device backup) may include this data according to your device settings. We do not have access to those backups.

Information collected automatically

To understand how the App is used and to fix problems, the App uses the following services from Google Firebase:

  • Firebase Analytics – anonymous usage information such as screens viewed, app opens, session length, app version, device model, operating system and approximate country (derived from your IP address). This is linked to a random app instance identifier, not to your name or email.
  • Firebase Crashlytics – crash reports containing technical information about the error, device model, operating system and app version.
  • Firebase Remote Config – lets us adjust app settings without an update. It uses the same random app instance identifier.

None of these services receive the content you enter in the App, such as your check-ins, journal entries, letters, drink days or goals. Google’s handling of this data is described in the Firebase Privacy and Security page.

Purchases and subscriptions

Optional Sober Tracker Plus subscriptions are purchased and billed through Apple’s App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment card details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier and your purchase history from the store.

Notifications

Reminders, milestone celebrations and letter notifications are scheduled locally on your device. You can change or turn them off at any time in the App’s settings or in your device settings.

Exporting and deleting your data

  • Export: Settings → Export my data creates a file on your device that you choose where to share or save.
  • Import: you can restore a file you exported earlier. It is read on your device only.
  • Delete: Settings → Delete all data permanently erases everything stored by the App on your device. Deleting the App also removes this data.

Because your journey data is never uploaded, we cannot recover it for you if it is deleted.

How we use information

  • To provide the App’s features on your device
  • To understand which features are used and improve the App
  • To detect, investigate and fix crashes and errors
  • To verify subscription status

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Health information

Information about your drinking, mood and cravings is sensitive. It stays on your device and is not shared with us, advertisers or any third party.

Children

The App is intended for adults. It is not directed to anyone under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information and to object to certain processing. Since your journey data is stored only on your device, you can view, export or delete it directly in the App. For questions about data collected by the services above, contact us using the email below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it was last changed.
